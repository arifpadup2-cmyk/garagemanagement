# ERP-Standard Audit — 2026-07-26

Full-system audit (features + UI) of Tecido Garage Management against ERP standards.
Four dimensions audited: module/feature gaps, transactional integrity, UI consistency, backend/validation.

## Verdict

The app is a solid **workshop operations tool** (job cards, kanban, technician portal, invoicing,
AR ledger) with a modern enterprise shell. It is **not yet ERP-standard** on three axes:

1. **Document chain has holes at both ends** — no quotation/estimate, appointments don't convert
   to job cards, and the entire purchase side (suppliers, PO, GRN, bills) is absent.
2. **Inventory is decorative** — parts never touch job cards or invoices; stock, COGS and margin
   are unmeasured. Stock adjust + payment recording are lost-update races.
3. **Financial & governance hygiene is thin** — no VAT engine, single-sided "double entry",
   trial balance/balance sheet structurally cannot balance, no audit trail, no users/roles,
   unauthenticated API, hard-delete only, no backup/export.
